Pillar one · Relaxing

Full-fill

3-10 minutes · Sitting or lying · Nose in, sigh out

Foundational breath taken up a level. You stack breaths before the hold, and that takes you further down than a single round does.

Breathing shallowAnxiousCan't sleep

The pattern

  1. 01Breathe in through the nose: belly, chest, head. Let it go.
  2. 02Second big breath, the same way. Let it go.
  3. 03Third big breath. This time fill all the way up.
  4. 04Hold 10 seconds at the top.
  5. 05Sigh it out, or use a long Ujjayi exhale.

Three breaths is where to start. More breaths before the hold makes it stronger. Seven, ten, fifteen. Change the number until it suits you.

If your chest moves before your belly, the order is backwards. Hand below the navel and start there.

How it lands

The stack does something a single breath does not. By the hold you are already further down than one round of foundational breath takes you, and the sigh finishes it.

What it does

  • Goes deeper than a single round of foundational breath
  • Settles the fight-or-flight response
  • Slows the heart rate
  • Scales: more breaths before the hold, stronger effect
  • Lowers blood pressure and brings inflammation down

If you want a hand with it

This one works on your own. Some of it lands harder with someone in the room, and that is what the workshops are for.
