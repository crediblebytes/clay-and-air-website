Pillar one · Relaxing

Turtle breath

5-10+ minutes · Lying down · Nose in, nose out

Deep relaxation and sleep. You slow the breath down in stages rather than dropping straight to the bottom.

Can't sleep

The pattern

  1. 01Run a few rounds of foundational breath first. You cannot reach the long counts cold.
  2. 02Lying down. Inhale through the nose, belly first, then chest, filling all the way up.
  3. 03Release from the top down with an Ujjayi exhale.
  4. 04Start at 10 seconds in, 10 seconds out.
  5. 05Move to 12, then 15, then 18, then 20 as each one stays comfortable.
  6. 06Stay at whatever count you can hold without effort.

Turtle breath is not the 20 seconds. It is the slowing down. Every breath a little longer than the last, and the number is where you end up, not where you start.

If a longer count starts to feel like work, or you find yourself needing the next breath, you have gone past it. Drop back a step. This is meant to settle you, not stress you.

How it lands

It arrives in stages rather than all at once. Each step up the ladder takes a bit more of the day with it, and the last few rounds are the ones that put you out.

What it does

  • Slows the breath until the nervous system unwinds
  • Built for lying down at the end of the day
  • Settles the fight-or-flight response
  • Slows the heart rate
  • Lowers blood pressure and brings inflammation down

If you want a hand with it

This one works on your own. Some of it lands harder with someone in the room, and that is what the workshops are for.
