02 · Get ready

What to bring, and how to arrive.

What to bring

A water bottle, a pillow, a blanket, an eye mask, and lip balm, because the breathing dries your mouth out. If we are at my place, bring something to wear in the ice plunge and a towel.

Come clear

No alcohol or drugs beforehand. I want you to have the real experience, with nothing in your system but you. Keep taking anything you are prescribed, and list it on the form.

Eat early

Nothing to eat for at least 90 minutes before. Three hours is better. The breathing is easier on an empty stomach.

Bring an intention

Something you want to let go of, or something you want to bring in. It does not have to be big. It gives the session a direction.

Afterwards

A slow way back, food, and a share circle around the fire. Nobody has to speak. Leave the rest of the day open, and read the days after when you get home.

Questions

Ask me anything before you come. Reply to any email from me and it comes straight to me.
