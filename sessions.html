Sessions

This is the part I do in person.

The library gives you practices you can use on your own. In person, I can see when you are forcing, holding back, or ready to go further. I guide the pace, help you stay with what comes up, and leave enough time for you to land before you walk back into your life. That is what a screen cannot do.

Three workshops. Two run an hour and need nothing but a room. The third takes an afternoon. All of them travel, so a gym, a studio or a retreat can host one.

Centering · Foundational breathwork

Nothing to believe in. It still works at 3am.

You already breathe. This is just doing it on purpose.

Format
1 hour. 8 to 25 people. Any time of day.
For
Anyone. No experience or flexibility needed.
Flow
Handpan while people settle. Foundational breath, then box breathing, then cleansing breath lying down with tuning forks.
You provide
A quiet room, floor space, low light if you have it.
I bring
Speaker, tuning forks, handpan, spare mats.

It sounds soft. It is.

Slow nasal breathing with short holds. If you have a heart or lung condition, high or low blood pressure, epilepsy, or you are pregnant, talk to a doctor before holding your breath.

First Fire · Dynamic breathwork

Yes, it is breathwork. No, nobody hugs you.

Forty hard breaths and two holds. Then you find out.

Format
1 hour. 8 to 20 people. Morning or afternoon.
For
Anyone cleared to hold their breath. No experience needed.
Flow
Handpan and resin while people settle. Foundational breath, then Dynamic breathwork, then cleansing breath flat on the floor.
You provide
Floor space and room for everyone to lie flat.
I bring
Speaker, tuning forks, handpan, resin, spare mats.

It sounds soft. It is not.

Fast, active breathing with two breath holds. Tell me in advance about fainting, panic attacks, pregnancy, blood pressure, epilepsy, or any heart or lung condition.

The Kiln · Sonic Neural Breathwork

Fifty five minutes with your eyes covered.

The rest of the three and a half hours is arriving and coming back.

Format
3.5 hours. 6 to 15 people. Afternoon start.
For
Screened in advance. Intake form and waiver required.
Flow
An hour to arrive and set intentions. Fifty five minutes of Sonic Neural Breathwork. Then a slow way back, food, and a share circle nobody has to speak in.
You provide
A private room, low light, restrooms, floor space.
I bring
Sound system, tuning forks, handpan, resin, a second space holder.

Most people only need this once a year.

Everyone completes an intake form and signs a waiver before this one. Screening covers heart conditions, epilepsy, eye conditions, pregnancy, blood thinners and panic attacks.

Why I teach this

I know what it is to watch the life I spent decades building become unrecognizable. Over a short stretch, I buried people I loved, lost my marriage, my financial foundation, nearly every relationship I had, and the day-to-day life I knew with my kids. I found myself in a spare bedroom, starting again from scratch, while the responsibility to work, provide, and keep going remained.

I found breathwork through plant medicine. Plant medicine saved me. Breathwork empowered me by showing me I could bring my body into a deep meditative state with nothing but my own breath. It became a practice I could return to anywhere, even at three in the morning when there was no one to call. When almost everything else was gone, my breath was still mine. That is why I teach it.
