Integration

The days after.

Sonic Neural keeps working after you get up off the floor. Things can surface for days: a memory, a mood, a dream that will not let go. That does not mean something went wrong. It means it is still moving. This is how to give it room.

Go easy on yourself

You may feel open, tender or raw for a while. Make time to look after yourself. Let whatever comes up come up, without judging it, and ease back into ordinary life slowly. The hard feelings are often where the insight is.

Look after your body

Rest. Drink plenty of water. Eat real food. Take a bath. Walk, stretch and move gently. Get outside, bare feet on the ground if you can, and meet the sun in the morning and the evening.

Stay with what came up

Notice the memories and feelings that surface over the next few days. Write things down, the night of the session and after it. Keep a notebook by the bed for dreams. Put music on and move. Share it with people you trust, and be choosy about who that is. The grounding practice on the Sonic Neural page helps, and so does anything in the relaxing pillar.

Hold off on big decisions

Give it at least a week before you make any major decision. Talk it over first with someone who will tell you the truth.

Your emotions are on your side

What you feel is yours. When something stirs you up, ask what in you it is pointing at, rather than what out there caused it. That is where the work is.

Protect your energy

A few days away from screens and the news, if you can. Some distance from people who drain you. Simple, whole food that grounds you. Water. And sleep. Get plenty of sleep.

If it stays heavy

Tell me. Reply to any email from me and it comes straight to me. If something is still heavy after a few days, talk to a therapist or your doctor as well. If it ever feels like too much, call or text 988.

Keep breathing

The relaxing pillar is the daily practice that holds all of this together.
