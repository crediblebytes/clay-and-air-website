Pillar two · Activating

Buteyko breathing

15-25 steps a round · Walking · Nose only

The one you can run while you are moving. Empty the lungs, hold, and walk. Use it on the way somewhere when you are wound up.

AnxiousUnder pressure

The pattern

  1. 01Breathe in through the nose.
  2. 02Exhale all the way out through the nose.
  3. 03Pinch your nose closed and hold.
  4. 04Walk. Fifteen to twenty-five steps.
  5. 05Let go, take one good breath in, and let it go.
  6. 06Repeat as you walk.

Pick a stretch of pavement where you are not crossing roads. Walking with your breath held is not the time to be watching traffic.

Altitude is the other use. Build the held walk up to 25, 30, 40 steps over weeks. If you live at sea level, doing this before a trip into the mountains is worth the effort.

If you are gasping when you let go, the walk was too long. Cut it back until you finish each round calm.

How it lands

It pulls you out of your head and into your legs. By the second or third hold the chatter has stopped and you are just counting steps.

What it does

  • Runs while you are moving, so it fits a walk to work
  • Gets you out of your head and into your body
  • Builds tolerance for altitude and effort over time
  • A small deliberate stress, the useful kind
  • Boosts immunity and brings inflammation down

If you want a hand with it

This one works on your own. Some of it lands harder with someone in the room, and that is what the workshops are for.
