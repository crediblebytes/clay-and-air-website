Pillar two · Activating

Priming breath

30 reps a set · Sitting or standing · Nose in, nose out

Wakes the system up. Mornings, before training, or any time you would otherwise reach for coffee.

FlatBefore training

Before you start

  • This starts a stress response on purpose. That is the point of it, and it is why it belongs in the morning rather than before bed.
  • Finish with something from the relaxing pillar. Foundational breath is enough.
  • Breathing this hard can make you lightheaded. Sit down to do it, and stop if your head goes.

The pattern

  1. 01Sit or stand with room to move your arms.
  2. 02Take a couple of ordinary breaths first, in through the nose and out through the mouth.
  3. 03Forceful inhale through the nose. Belly pushes out, arms go up overhead.
  4. 04Forceful exhale through the nose. Belly pulls in, arms come down with your elbows tucked to your sides.
  5. 05Keep the arms and the breath on the same beat. Thirty reps is one set.
  6. 06As many sets as you want. Then sit still for a few minutes.

It should feel mechanical, like a piston. The force is in both halves of the breath, not just the exhale.

Lightheaded means you are moving more air than you need. Slow the rate down, keep the force, and sit if you are not already sitting.

How it lands

You feel it fast. Warm, awake, and buzzing slightly, the way you do after the first minute of a hard walk.

What it does

  • Gets the blood moving
  • Expands and contracts the lungs hard
  • Wakes you up without reaching for coffee
  • Leaves you alert enough to sit and meditate afterwards
  • Boosts immunity and brings inflammation down

The rest of this pillar

  • Buteyko breathing 15-25 steps a round · Walking A held walk. Runs while you are moving.
  • Kapalabhati 50 reps or 2-7 minutes · Sitting upright Forceful exhale only. Works the stomach.
  • Igniter breath 30 seconds to 5 minutes · Sitting upright Warms you up fast. After cold water, or before training.
  • Twisting breath About 10 minutes · Sitting or standing Twist and exhale hard. Warm-up that works the core.
  • Heart opening breath 10-20 minutes · Sitting or standing Loud on the exhale. Clears the deck before the day.
  • Kundalini Bhastrika 30 seconds to 5 minutes · Sitting or standing Breath of fire, arms overhead. Harder.
  • Digestion igniter 10-15 minutes · Standing Lion's breath and a stomach vacuum. Standing.
  • Dynamic breathwork About 20 minutes · Lying down or sitting Forty breaths and holds. The big one in this pillar.
