The library

What these are

Breathing patterns you can run with nothing you do not already have. You have probably tried one of them badly under pressure and found it did nothing. Most of them take three minutes when you run them properly.

Every technique here is written so you can follow it alone, at the point you need it, without reading an essay first. Read one all the way through once while you are calm. Then it is there at 3am.

Where these come from

Ben Holt

Every technique on this site is one of his, learned through Awakened Breath and the 21 Day Breathwork Academy. The three pillars are his framework. The counts, the mechanics and the order they are taught in are his. Two of them are his own designs: Dynamic breathwork, built on the Wim Hof method, and Sonic Neural.

What is ours is the wording and the way it is arranged here, so you can find the right one quickly and follow it on your own. If you want the source, go to him: awakenedbreath.org.
