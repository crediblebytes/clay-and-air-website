Workshops

This is the part I do in person.

The library gives you practices you can use on your own. In person, I can see when you are forcing, holding back, or ready to go further. I guide the pace, help you stay with what comes up, and leave enough time for you to land before you walk back into your life. That is what a screen cannot do.

Three workshops. Centering runs an hour, First Fire an hour and a half. The Kiln takes an afternoon or an evening. I host any of them at my place, or I bring one to yours: a gym, a studio, a farm, a retreat.

Foundational breathwork

Centering

Nothing to believe in. It still works at 3am.

You already breathe. This is just doing it on purpose.

1 hour · 4 to 15 people · Any time of day

Ask about Centering

For
Anyone. No experience or flexibility needed.
Flow
Foundational breath, then box breathing, then cleansing breath lying down with tuning forks.
You provide
A quiet room, floor space, low light if you have it.
I bring
Speaker, tuning forks, spare mats.

Slow nasal breathing with short holds. If you have a heart or lung condition, high or low blood pressure, epilepsy, or you are pregnant, talk to a doctor before holding your breath.

Dynamic breathwork

First Fire

Yes, it is breathwork. No, nobody hugs you.

Forty hard breaths and two holds. Then you find out.

1.5 hours · 4 to 10 people · Morning or afternoon

Ask about First Fire

For
Anyone cleared to hold their breath. No experience needed.
Flow
Resin while people settle. Foundational breath, then Dynamic breathwork, then cleansing breath flat on the floor.
You provide
Floor space and room for everyone to lie flat.
I bring
Speaker, tuning forks, resin, spare mats.

Fast, active breathing with two breath holds. Tell me in advance about fainting, panic attacks, pregnancy, blood pressure, epilepsy, or any heart or lung condition.

Sonic Neural Breathwork

The Kiln

Fifty five minutes with your eyes covered.

The rest of the two and a half hours is arriving and coming back.

2.5 hours · 3 to 5 people · Afternoon or evening

Ask about The Kiln Before The Kiln

For
Screened in advance. Intake form and waiver required.
Flow
Arrive to handpan and set intentions. Fifty five minutes of Sonic Neural Breathwork, a holotropic style of breathwork. Then a slow way back and a share circle that nobody has to speak in.
You provide
A private room, low light, restrooms and floor space.
I bring
Sound system, tuning forks, handpan, resin, and a second space holder for groups of 6 to 10.

Everyone completes an intake form and signs a waiver before this one. Screening covers heart conditions, epilepsy, eye conditions, pregnancy, blood thinners and panic attacks.

Why breathe

I did everything I was supposed to do. I served a mission, built a career, and provided for my family. Then I started questioning all of it, and in 2019 I left the church I was raised in.

That was the foundation everything else stood on. When it went, the rest followed. Over a short stretch, I buried my father and others I loved, lost my marriage, my financial foundation, nearly every relationship I had, and the day-to-day life I knew with my kids. The people closest to me treated me like I was the one who had lost it. I found myself in a spare bedroom, starting again from scratch, God included, while the responsibility to work, provide, and keep going remained.

I found plant medicine, and it saved me. Eventually that led me to breathwork, which empowered me by showing me I could bring my body into a deep meditative state with nothing but my own breath.

The rest came slowly. Eating clean. Time outside, in the woods and around a fire. Breathing on purpose. I carried a lot of anger through all of it. I have felt the pain, and I have felt the peace that comes after it. I want to help anyone who lost their purpose find meaning again.

What happens next

I read every message myself and reply within a couple of days. We settle the date, the place and who is coming, and I send you what to expect and what to bring. For The Kiln, the intake form and waiver come first.
